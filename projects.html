The work.

Boards, cores, ovens, and machines — each one taken from idea to a working system.

01 · RF / PCB

NFC business card

13.56 MHz Altium Planar antenna

An NFC-enabled business card PCB using inductive coupling at 13.56 MHz. I designed a custom planar antenna with optimized trace geometry to hit target inductance for resonant operation, then validated it with electromagnetic simulation and parametric spreadsheet modeling.

Schematic and layout were completed in Altium Designer, including custom libraries and footprints, with an NFC controller, power management, and firmware for contactless data.

02 · Digital design

32-bit RISC machine

Verilog ModelSim DE1-SoC

A 32-bit RISC processor in Verilog HDL with instruction decode, FSM control, ALU, and dual-port memory. Testbenches in ModelSim covered function and timing; synthesis targeted an Altera DE1-SoC in Quartus Prime.

The design ran on-board with real-time instruction execution and memory operations — RTL, verification, and FPGA bring-up in one stack.

03 · Embedded

Arcade claw

Arduino Ultrasonic PWM servo

Embedded control for an automated arcade claw on Arduino. Ultrasonic ranging with calibrated thresholds positioned the grab; servo actuation used tuned PWM timing for repeatable cycles.

Power was protected and current-limited. The build was a full mech-electrical integration with a multidisciplinary team.

04 · Thermal systems

Reflow solder oven

Nuvoton PWM heat Python DAQ

A dual-microcontroller reflow oven with precision temperature profiling. Master-slave signaling between Nuvoton MCUs coordinated thermal control. Assembly firmware ran the FSM, LCD, and inter-processor protocol.

Timer-based PWM closed the loop on thermocouple feedback. A Python acquisition stack plotted live profiles, with buzzer, LEDs, and serial status on the side.

05 · Robotics

Coin-picking robot

Vision PID Sensor fusion

An autonomous collector with computer-vision detection and a servo gripper. Ultrasonic and optical sensors fused for localization under changing light; PID loops handled drive and path tracking.

Battery monitoring and low-power modes kept the platform honest — robotics as systems work, not a demo on a table.
